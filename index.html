About Me

I am an AWS Cloud and DevOps Engineer with 1.5 years of hands-on AWS cloud operations experience. I spent that time monitoring EC2 environments, troubleshooting Linux issues, and writing runbooks that cut repeat escalations by 35%.

Outside of work I build production-style projects on Amazon EKS. They cover Terraform-provisioned infrastructure, GitHub Actions CI/CD with Trivy and SonarQube scans, Helm releases, ALB Ingress with HTTPS, autoscaling, and Prometheus and Grafana monitoring. Each one is documented step by step so anyone can reproduce it.

Before moving into cloud operations I spent over two years in MIS reporting, where I learned to validate data, track SLAs, and document fixes as SOPs. I bring that habit of precision and documentation into my infrastructure work.

I am looking for an AWS Cloud / DevOps Engineer role focused on cloud infrastructure, automation, Kubernetes and CI/CD.
